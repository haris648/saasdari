Farms & technology

Shade-net houses built for the Gulf sun

Our growing houses soften the desert sun, keep pests out and put water straight at the root, so food can grow close to the people who eat it.

  1. Frame
  2. Shade net
  3. Water
  4. Growth
Steel frameGalvanised arches that stand up to desert wind and heat.
